# Alternatives, gifts and what to buy next

For a perfume the person doesn't have yet: something similar, cheaper, a dupe, an upgrade, a
twist, a gift, a fill for a gap on their shelf. The method: **the web and your knowledge give
names; Tell Index checks them.** Every perfume you recommend has been read in Tell Index, and what
you say about it comes from its profile.

## 1. The anchor and the wish

- Anchor perfume: `fragrance_search` → `fragrance_profile` — what makes it itself (summary,
  notes noticed first, top accords, Tell and zone, where people wear it). Several anchors: their
  shared character is the taste; profile one or two representative ones.
- The wish, from their words: cheaper, the same feel but less common, quieter or louder, another
  season, longer lasting, a twist, a gift (who, what they like, budget, market). Infer it and say
  what you went for; ask one short question only when it's wide open.
- Their own shelf: `wardrobe_list` (sorted by rating; each bottle's `wear`) or the gaps from `wardrobe_verdict`.

## 2. Candidates

- Connected account: their liked and want-to-try lists are the first source — perfumes they
  already chose.
- The web: 2–4 searches in English and their language (alternatives, dupes, "smells like … but
  cheaper", best-of lists for the style or season). Editorial round-ups, retailer and brand pages
  and community threads give names; perfume facts come from `fragrance_profile`.
- Your own knowledge. Collect 6–10 names, then narrow.

## 3. Checked in Tell Index

For the 3–5 strongest names: `fragrance_search` (exact name and concentration) →
`fragrance_profile`. Keep a candidate when its profile supports the wish:

- similar → summary, notes noticed first and top accords overlap the anchor's;
- cheaper → similar, and a lower price in their market (`fragrance_prices` for the anchor and
  your top pick);
- quieter / louder → its Tell against the anchor's;
- season, occasion, heat → where it peaks, day/night, `careful`, where people wear it;
- gift → easy to love (most people love or like it; without `feeling`, its labels and debates
  tell you), true to the hints, and not so common that it feels unchosen.

The profile wins over the web. A name `fragrance_search` doesn't find stays out. Re-poll
`pending` only for the picks you present.

## 4. Present 2–3 picks

- **Name**, its Tell Index page as a link of its own; one sentence on how it relates to the
  anchor or the wish, from its profile, with its Tell line when it lands. Start from the picks —
  they know their anchor.
- Tell · zone for each, set against the anchor or the other picks by what people around will
  notice and from how far.
- One price per pick (store, size) when money is part of the wish.
- Who a pick is marketed to, when the person's gender is unknown and that might matter.
- Best match first, and say which one you'd start with.

## 5. The offer

- Connected: want-to-try (`want_sample`), like, or a price target when it costs more than they
  want to pay. Act on yes.
- Not connected: prices for the finalist, a sample first; when they want to keep the shortlist,
  connecting their Tell Index account.
- A gift: prices, a sample to test on the person first, or comparing the two finalists.
- The page reason: its Dupes tab has Tell Index's own cheaper / upgrade / different picks; the
  Comparison tab puts two side by side.
