# tellindex.com pages and the reason to open each

Use the `url` the tools return. A perfume `url` (`https://tellindex.com/<slug>`) opens in the
reader's language. The wardrobe is `https://tellindex.com/<lang>/wardrobe`; a screen is added
with `?tab=` or `?zone=dark`. `<lang>` is `uk`, `ru`, `en`, `de` or `es` — the person's language.
Name a button the way the site shows it in their language (table below).

## Perfume page — `url` from any perfume tool

| They asked about… | The page gives them |
|---|---|
| how loud, how it'll be on them | a **personal Tell** recalculated for their city, today's weather, their dose and the occasion |
| where they can wear it | "Where can you wear it?" — office, date, club, heat, each with what to do |
| what it smells like, notes | **perceived** notes next to the brand's **declared** notes, accord bars, how it unfolds |
| price, where to buy | the Price tab: every store by size with **buy links**, crossed-out old prices, samples and decants, Amazon, and **Track the discount** in one tap |
| is it fake | the Antifake tab: risk level, what to check, and a **photo check** of the bottle, box and batch code |
| what people think | the Reviews tab: where people agree and where they split, with quotes, and a 30-second **vote** on how it wears on them |
| similar or cheaper | the Dupes tab: Tell Index's own cheaper / upgrade / different picks, each with "choose it if…", plus perfumes similar by composition |
| A or B | the Comparison tab builds a **duel page**: the two side by side, how similar they are, shared and distinct notes, today's prices with buy buttons — made to screenshot and send |
| allergens | the Allergens tab: which EU-listed allergens it likely contains |
| keeping it | like it, "I own this" or "I have a sample" — straight into their wardrobe; share it, or send someone a portrait of what it says about them |

## Wardrobe — `https://tellindex.com/<lang>/wardrobe`

| Screen | Link | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Volume | `?tab=dial` (default) | their shelf on a loudness dial, each bottle a dot; drag the needle through the zones |
| What you collected | `?tab=about` | the quick read of the shelf (value, loudness, duplicates, gaps) and stories about it |
| Money | `?tab=money` | what the shelf is worth, money asleep in unworn bottles, near-duplicates, what runs out first, where it's cheaper; mark how much is left on a bottle photo |
| For the occasion | `?tab=look` | a pick from their shelf by a **photo of their outfit**, or by scene and weather |
| Samples | `?tab=lab` | samples being tested, the want-to-try list, picks for the shelf's gaps |
| Archive | `?tab=arch` | waiting for a discount (price targets with goals and buy links), liked, viewing history |
| What they hear (dark zone) | `?zone=dark` | the shelf as real bottle photos; which bottles get asked about; archetype verdicts per occasion; **Today** with "I'll take this"; shareable 9:16 cards of the shelf and the scent of the day |

Adding many perfumes at once is fastest on the site: one photo of the whole shelf, a screenshot,
or a TikTok / Reels / YouTube link.
